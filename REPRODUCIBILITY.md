# Reproducibility Guide

## 1. Purpose

This document describes how to reproduce the computational experiments associated with:

**Reliability-Aware Multi-View Forensic Evidence Synthesis for Network Intrusion Investigation**

The repository is organized to preserve traceability between the experimental implementation, experiment-specific outputs, consolidated results, and the results reported in the associated manuscript.

The intended reproducibility chain is:

```text
Official Dataset Sources
        ↓
Original Downloaded Data
        ↓
Dataset Preparation
        ↓
Semantic Evidence Representation
        ↓
Model Training and Framework Evaluation
        ↓
Experiment-Specific Outputs
        ↓
Statistical and Computational Evaluation
        ↓
Final Results Consolidation
